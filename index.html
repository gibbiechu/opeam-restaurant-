The Story

"Less is more — reliant purely on light touch,
seasonings, and a discerning palate."

— Chef Nicole Gan, Founder

Opëam is the brainchild of young chef-entrepreneur Nicole Gan, from Seremban. After obtaining a degree in mass communications, Nicole took off for Los Angeles, New York and Philadelphia — where she developed a deep passion for baking and an eye for quality produce. Returning to Ipoh, she opened Patisserie Boutique in Old Town, quickly garnering a faithful following for its delectable pastries and simple fare.

Now, with Opëam, Nicole has moved into fine food and beverage in a dramatic way. Housed in a discreet bungalow behind a quietly lit sign, the restaurant embodies the Californian philosophy she absorbed abroad — menus that are light, minimal in oil, and reliant purely on the natural flavours of exceptional ingredients. Despite no formal chef's training, her beautifully plated dishes speak for themselves.
